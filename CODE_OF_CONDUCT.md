# Code of Conduct

[日本語](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT-ja.md)

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community.
Be considerate, focus criticism on ideas and code, respect privacy, and assume
good intent while remaining accountable for impact.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private information, and
other conduct that makes participation unsafe are not acceptable. Maintainers
may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository
owner's contact channel. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue.
